Frequently asked questions

What is Heartlog?

Heartlog is a dating journal app that helps you track your dating life, remember important details about the people you're dating, and get personalized advice from Amie, your AI-powered dating advisor.

What is Amie?

Amie is your personal AI dating advisor within Heartlog. She can help you with dating advice, suggest conversation topics, and provide insights based on your dating journal entries. Note: Amie provides dating guidance only and is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health advice.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your personal dating journal entries and notes are stored securely and are never shared with third parties. Please review the Privacy Policy for complete details.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app by going to Settings → Delete Account. If you need assistance, contact heartlogdatingjournal@gmail.com.

How do Amie credits and Unlimited work?

New accounts receive a small number of free Amie insights to try the AI features. After that, you can continue with a one-time credit pack, or subscribe to Unlimited Amie (monthly or annual). Annual plans may include a free trial; cancel anytime in App Store settings before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Current prices are on the pricing section of the home page.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel (including during a free trial):

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap "Subscriptions"
  • Select Heartlog and tap "Cancel Subscription"

I'm having technical issues with the app

Email heartlogdatingjournal@gmail.com with:

  • A description of the issue
  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15)
  • Your iOS version
  • Screenshots if applicable

You can also use Report a Bug inside the app.

Can I request a feature?

Yes. Send feature requests to heartlogdatingjournal@gmail.com and I'll consider them for future updates. Recent releases are listed in the changelog.

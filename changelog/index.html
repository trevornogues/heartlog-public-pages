Changelog

What shipped, and when. Newest first.

1.7 August 10, 2026

  • Guided first-prospect setup. A short walkthrough the first time you add someone.
  • Better reminders.
  • A sharper Amie.
  • Third-date check-in. A short prompt to help you decide whether to keep seeing someone.

1.6 April 20, 2026

  • Multiple photos on dates and memories.
  • Shareable date cards, monthly recaps, and dating journey timelines continue from 1.5.

1.5 April 11, 2026

  • Shareable date cards and monthly recaps. Turn a date or a month's highlights into a card you can send or paste into a text. You choose what's visible.
  • Dating journey timelines for singles. Your connections, dates, and memorable moments as one personal timeline.
  • Design refinements throughout.

1.4 March 16, 2026

  • New look. A full visual refresh: cleaner, smoother, easier to use.
  • Weekly community polls. Vote on a dating question each week and see anonymous results from other Heartlog users.
  • Partner timelines. Your relationship story in one chronological view of dates, memories, and milestones.
  • Improved partner and prospect profiles.

1.3 February 23, 2026

  • Amie conversation summaries. Tap Save in a chat and Amie summarizes it into your Daily Journal.
  • Monthly recap. A snapshot of your journaling each month, with optional insights from Amie.
  • Report a bug from inside the app.
  • Fixed an issue where the keyboard could not be dismissed.

1.2 February 16, 2026

  • One-time Amie credit pack. If a subscription is more commitment than you want, buy 20 additional Amie requests once.
  • General UI fixes.

1.1 February 6, 2026

  • Updated onboarding flow, which you can replay from Settings.
  • “Met on” date for prospects.
  • Fixed a bug when adding custom date categories.
  • UI improvements.

1.0 February 2, 2026

First release on the App Store.

  • Prospects with profiles, an Active tab, and a Graveyard for connections that ended.
  • Dates on an integrated calendar, with memories, reflections, and photos after each one.
  • Daily journal entries.
  • Committed-relationship mode for tracking quality time and milestones with a partner.
  • Amie, a built-in AI advisor who can reference your dates, prospects, and entries. Optional, and can be turned off in Settings.
  • JSON export and in-app account deletion. Day and night modes.

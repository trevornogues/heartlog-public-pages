Questions people ask

What does Amie see?

When you ask Amie something, your message and limited context from your journal (recent dates, prospects, entries) are sent to OpenAI to generate the reply. Heartlog does not save or log the chat, and data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train their models. You can turn Amie off in Settings and the journal works without her. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Heartlog is a personal journal, not a social network. Nobody else can see your entries. Your data is stored in your own account on Firebase (Google Cloud), is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used to train AI models. You can export everything as JSON or delete your account from Settings at any time.

Is Heartlog free?

Yes. The journal is free to download and use. New accounts also get a small number of free Amie insights. After that, Amie is the only paid part: a one-time pack of 20 insights for $3.99, or Unlimited Amie for $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

How do I cancel Unlimited Amie?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select Heartlog, and tap Cancel Subscription. If you cancel during a free trial you're not charged. The journal keeps working either way.

Is Amie a therapist?

No. Amie gives dating and relationship guidance only. She is not a licensed therapist, counselor, or medical professional, and Heartlog is not a substitute for professional care. If you have health or mental health concerns, please talk to a qualified provider.
